# Changelog

## 0.3.3 - 2026-11-17
- Add a `--desc` argument to narrow a run.

## 0.3.2 - 2026-11-14
- Safer project profile updates: edits are small, verified and shown as a one-line before and after.
  Branch- and ticket-specific facts stay out of the profile.

## 0.3.1 - 2026-11-14
- Open items and developer questions are written in plain conversational English.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-14
- A re-run finds an earlier tech spec (marker plus a fingerprint of the product text) and revises
  only what changed acceptance criteria affect.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-14
- Open items carry a consequence level and are ordered High to Low. Developer replies that refer to
  an item by description are resolved, and "keep current behaviour" rejections are applied.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-13
- Initial release of `tech-spec-writer`.
